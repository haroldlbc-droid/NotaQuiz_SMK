TOPIK 1 / TOPIC 1: 
4.1 Maksud Tamadun / 4.1 Meaning of Civilisation
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RINGKASAN TOPIK 1 / TOPIC 1 SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Bahasa Yunani (Greek): Berasal daripada "Civitas" (bandar/kota). Bermaksud 
  kemajuan dalam kebudayaan, sains, industri, dan sistem kerajaan. / Derived 
  from "Civitas" (town/city). Means progress in culture, science, industry, 
  and government system.
* Bahasa Inggeris (English): "Civilisation" merujuk kepada peringkat 
  perkembangan manusia dan organisasi yang dianggap paling maju. / 
  "Civilisation" refers to the stage of human and organizational development 
  considered most advanced.
* Bahasa Arab (Arabic): Berasal daripada "Mudun", "Madain", "Madana" (budi 
  pekerti tinggi & pembukaan bandar). "Hadharah" merujuk kepada kawasan 
  pertanian/bandar, manakala "Hadari" merujuk kepada tanah/rumah tempat 
  tinggal & kekayaan. / Derived from "Mudun", "Madain", "Madana" (high manners 
  & opening of cities). "Hadharah" refers to farming region/city, while 
  "Hadari" refers to settled land/house & wealth.
* Bahasa Melayu (Malay): "Peradaban" bermaksud kemajuan tinggi dalam kebendaan 
  (seni bina, ekonomi) dan pemikiran. / "Peradaban" means high progress in 
  material (architecture, economy) and thought.

SOALAN SUBJEKTIF (1 SOALAN) / SUBJECTIVE QUESTION (1 QUESTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. Nyatakan perbezaan maksud tamadun mengikut sudut pandangan Bahasa Yunani 
    dan Bahasa Melayu.
    State the difference in the meaning of civilisation from the perspectives 
    of the Greek and Malay languages.
    JAWAPAN / ANSWER:
    Bahasa Yunani merujuk tamadun kepada perkataan "Civitas" yang bermaksud 
    bandar/kota serta kemajuan dalam kebudayaan, sains, industri, dan sistem 
    kerajaan. Manakala Bahasa Melayu merujuk tamadun sebagai "peradaban", iaitu 
    kemajuan tinggi dalam kebendaan (seni bina, ekonomi) dan pemikiran. / 
    Greek refers to civilisation via the word "Civitas" meaning town/city and 
    progress in culture, science, industry, and government system. Meanwhile, 
    Malay refers to civilisation as "peradaban", meaning high progress in 
    material (architecture, economy) and thought.

TOPIK 2 / TOPIC 2: 
4.2 Konsep Tamadun (Islam & Barat) / 4.2 Concept of Civilisation (Islam & West)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RINGKASAN TOPIK 2 / TOPIC 2 SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Pandangan Islam (Islamic View): Dibina atas asas Islam sebagai al-Din (cara 
  hidup). Menekankan keseimbangan perkembangan lahiriah (fizikal) dan rohaniah 
  berdasarkan al-Quran dan Hadis. / Built upon Islam as al-Din (way of life). 
  Emphasizes a balance of physical and spiritual development based on Quran & 
  Hadith.
* Ibn Khaldun: Memperkenalkan istilah "Umran" dan "Hadharah" dalam *Muqaddimah*. 
  Hadharah merangkumi akidah, undang-undang, akhlak, falsafah, dan budaya, 
  manakala Madaniyyah merujuk kepada aspek kebendaan. / Introduced "Umran" and 
  "Hadharah" in *Muqaddimah*. Hadharah covers faith, law, morals, philosophy, 
  and culture, while Madaniyyah refers to material aspects.
* Syed Muhammad Naquib Al-Attas: Tamadun ialah pencapaian kehalusan budi 
  pekerti dan kebudayaan yang mulia. / Civilisation is the achievement of 
  refinement in manners and noble culture.
* Pandangan Barat (Western View): Berfokus utama kepada pembangunan lahiriah/
  kebendaan (sains, teknologi, ekonomi, pembandaran). Contohnya, Arnold J. 
  Toynbee mendefinisikan tamadun sebagai sistem yang memperkasakan sistem 
  politik, ekonomi, sosial, dan seni. / Focuses primarily on material 
  development (science, technology, economics, urbanization). E.g., Arnold 
  J. Toynbee defined civilisation as a system that empowers political, 
  economic, social, and artistic systems.

TOPIK 3 / TOPIC 3: 
4.3 Ciri-ciri Tamadun Awal Dunia / 4.3 Characteristics of Early Civilisations
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RINGKASAN TOPIK 3 / TOPIC 3 SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Pertanian & Perdagangan / Agriculture & Trade: Inovasi alatan & pengairan; 
  perdagangan barter tempatan dan jarak jauh untuk logam, emas, besi. / Tool & 
  irrigation innovations; local barter and long-distance trade for metals, 
  gold, iron.
* Sistem Pemerintahan / Government System: Kepimpinan beralih daripada 
  pendeta kepada Raja; undang-undang diwujudkan. / Leadership shifted from 
  priests to Kings; laws were enacted.
* Pembentukan Bandar / Formation of Cities: Evolusi kampung menjadi bandar 
  kekal berstruktur dengan kubu dan kuil (Ur, Uruk, Memphis, Mohenjo-Daro). / 
  Villages evolved into structured permanent cities with forts and temples (Ur, 
  Uruk, Memphis, Mohenjo-Daro).
* Pengkhususan Pekerjaan / Job Specialisation: Peranan khusus seperti pemungut 
  cukai, askar, jurutera, petani, dan tukang. / Specialized roles like tax 
  collectors, soldiers, engineers, farmers, and artisans.
* Teknologi / Technology: Pembinaan monumen (Piramid, Sphinx) & reka cipta 
  (metalurgi, roda, bajak). / Monumental builds (Pyramids, Sphinx) & 
  inventions (metallurgy, wheels, plows).
* Organisasi Sosial / Social Organisation: Kelas berhirarki (Pemerintah/
  Bangsawan -> Tukang/Pedagang -> Petani -> Hamba). / Hierarchical classes 
  (Ruler/Nobles -> Artisans/Merchants -> Farmers -> Slaves).
* Agama & Kepercayaan / Religion & Beliefs: Amalan Politeisme (percaya banyak 
  tuhan) & kepercayaan kehidupan selepas mati. / Polytheism (belief in many 
  gods) & belief in life after death.
* Tulisan & Rekod / Writing & Record Keeping: Bermula dengan piktograf 
  (Kuneiform, tulang ramalan). / Started with pictographs (Cuneiform, oracle 
  bones).
* Kesenian & Kesusasteraan / Art & Literature: Arca, ukiran kaca, dan kisah 
  tuhan/pemerintah. / Sculptures, glass carvings, and god/ruler stories.
