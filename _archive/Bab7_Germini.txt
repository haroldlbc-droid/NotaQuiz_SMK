TOPIK 7.1: TAMADUN INDIA (INDIAN CIVILIZATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[RINGKASAN TOPIK / TOPIC SUMMARY]
(BM)
- Perluasan Kuasa: Merupakan usaha sesebuah kerajaan/raja bagi menguasai dan memperluas pengaruh di sesebuah kawasan.
- Faktor Perluasan Kuasa: Kekuatan ketenteraan, dasar pemerintahan, sumber manusia, diplomasi keagamaan, dan kewangan.
- Bentuk Perluasan Kuasa:
  1. Fizikal: Menguasai wilayah lain dengan kekuatan tentera dan disiplin. Dilihat dalam Dinasti Nanda (20,000 kavalri, 200,000 infantri, 3,000 tentera bergajah), Dinasti Maurya (Chandragupta Maurya & Asoka), dan Dinasti Gupta (Chandragupta I). Pataliputra merupakan pusat pemerintahan utama.
  2. Keagamaan: Merentas sempadan wilayah. Selepas Perang Kalinga yang mengorbankan 100,000 nyawa dan 150,000 kehilangan harta, Maharaja Asoka mengubah dasar kepada penyebaran agama Buddha (mengukuhkan undang-undang melalui Tiang Asoka). Misi dihantar ke Tibet, Nepal, Sri Lanka (oleh Mahendra), serta Asia Tenggara (oleh Sona dan Uttara).
  3. Zaman Gupta: Berasaskan agama Hindu (dikenali sebagai zaman keemasan agama Hindu). Raja Samudragupta dikenali sebagai Kaviraja (raja penyair).

(EN)
- Expansion of Power: Efforts by a kingdom or ruler to control and expand influence in a region.
- Factors of Expansion: Military strength, governance policy, human resources, religious diplomacy, and finance.
- Forms of Expansion:
  1. Physical: Dominating other territories through military strength and discipline. Seen in the Nanda Dynasty (20,000 cavalry, 200,000 infantry, 3,000 war elephants), Maurya Dynasty (Chandragupta Maurya & Asoka), and Gupta Dynasty (Chandragupta I). Pataliputra served as the main center of administration.
  2. Religious: Transcends territorial boundaries. After the Kalinga War, which claimed 100,000 lives and left 150,000 homeless, Emperor Asoka shifted policy to spreading Buddhism (inscribing laws on the Pillars of Asoka). Missions were sent to Tibet, Nepal, Sri Lanka (led by Mahendra), and Southeast Asia (led by Sona and Uttara).
  3. Gupta Period: Emphasized Hinduism (known as the golden age of Hinduism). King Samudragupta was known as Kaviraja (king of poets).

[SOALAN SUBJEKTIF / SUBJECTIVE QUESTION]

Soalan / Question:
(BM) Jelaskan perbezaan antara perluasan kuasa secara fizikal dengan perluasan kuasa secara keagamaan dalam Tamadun India.
(EN) Explain the difference between physical expansion and religious expansion in the Indian Civilization.

Jawapan / Answer:
(BM) 
1. Perluasan fizikal bergantung kepada kekuatan ketenteraan dan penguasaan sempadan wilayah secara mendadak/taklukan.
2. Perluasan keagamaan merentas sempadan wilayah tanpa menggunakan tentera, sebaliknya menumpukan kepada penyebaran nilai keagamaan, kemanusiaan, dan kedamaian (contohnya penyebaran agama Buddha oleh Asoka melalui Tiang Asoka dan misi diplomatik/keagamaan).

(EN)
1. Physical expansion relied on military power and the conquest of fixed territorial boundaries.
2. Religious expansion passed beyond physical borders without using military force, focusing instead on spreading religious values, humanity, and peace (for example, Asoka's propagation of Buddhism via the Asoka Pillars and diplomatic/religious missions).

TOPIK 7.2: TAMADUN CHINA (CHINESE CIVILIZATION)
================================================================================

[RINGKASAN TOPIK / TOPIC SUMMARY]
(BM)
- Lokasi & Perkembangan: Bermula di Lembah Sungai Huang He. 
- Dinasti Qin (221 SM - 206 SM): Berpusat di Xianyang. Maharaja Shi Huangdi menyatukan China, menyeragamkan tulisan, unit timbang dan sukat, serta membina Tembok Besar.
- Dinasti Han (206 SM - 220 M): Diasaskan oleh Liu Bang (Maharaja Gaozu). Zaman Han Awal berpusat di Chang'an dan Han Akhir di Loyang. Kemuncak pada zaman Maharaja Han Wu Di (pembukaan Laluan Sutera dan pelaksanaan peperiksaan awam).
- Matlamat Sistem Pendidikan: Lulus peperiksaan perkhidmatan awam, memupuk nilai moral/etika, memilih pegawai kerajaan, mengekalkan ajaran Konfusianisme, dan membezakan golongan elit dengan rakyat.
- Peringkat Pendidikan:
  1. Rendah: Menghafal tulisan dan buku suci.
  2. Menengah: Menulis karangan dan sajak.
  3. Tinggi: Menterjemah dan menginterpretasi buku suci (Sembilan Buku Suci / The Nine Sacred Books).
- Peperiksaan Perkhidmatan Awam:
  1. Xiucai (Tahap Pertama): Peringkat daerah, diadakan 2 kali setiap 3 tahun, ujian 1 hari.
  2. Juren (Tahap Kedua): Ibu kota daerah, 3 tahun sekali, ujian 3 hari.
  3. Jinshi (Tahap Ketiga): Ibu kota kerajaan, 3 tahun sekali, ujian 13 hari.
- Ciri-Ciri: Sangat ketat, hanya untuk lelaki, mengekalkan tradisi Konfusianisme, calon dikurung untuk elak meniru.

(EN)
- Location & Development: Emerged around the Huang He River Valley.
- Qin Dynasty (221 BC - 206 BC): Centered in Xianyang. Emperor Shi Huangdi unified China, standardized writing, weights and measures, and built the Great Wall.
- Han Dynasty (206 BC - 220 AD): Founded by Liu Bang (Emperor Gaozu). Early Han was centered in Chang'an and Later Han in Loyang. Reached its peak during Emperor Han Wu Di's reign (opening of the Silk Road and implementation of civil service exams).
- Goals of the Education System: Pass civil service examinations, foster moral/ethical values, select government officials, preserve Confucian teachings, and distinguish the elite class from commoners.
- Stages of Education:
  1. Primary: Memorizing scripts and sacred texts.
  2. Secondary: Composition writing and poetry.
  3. Higher: Translating and interpreting sacred texts (The Nine Sacred Books).
- Civil Service Examinations:
  1. Xiucai (First Level): District level, held twice every 3 years, 1-day exam.
  2. Juren (Second Level): Provincial capital, held every 3 years, 3-day exam.
  3. Jinshi (Third Level): Imperial capital, held every 3 years, 13-day exam.
- Features: Highly competitive and strict, restricted to men regardless of social background, maintained Confucian traditions, candidates were isolated to prevent cheating.

[SOALAN SUBJEKTIF / SUBJECTIVE QUESTION]

Soalan / Question:
(BM) Nyatakan TIGA ciri utama sistem peperiksaan perkhidmatan awam yang dilaksanakan dalam Tamadun China.
(EN) State THREE main characteristics of the civil service examination system implemented in the Chinese Civilization.

Jawapan / Answer:
(BM)
1. Peperiksaan adalah sangat kompetitif dan dikawal dengan ketat untuk mengelakkan penipuan (calon dikurung sepanjang peperiksaan).
2. Terbuka kepada semua golongan lelaki tanpa mengira latar belakang dan status sosial.
3. Berteraskan kepada kandungan ajaran Konfusianisme (Empat Buku dan Lima Kitab / Sembilan Buku Suci).

(EN)
1. The examinations were highly competitive and strictly controlled to prevent cheating (candidates were isolated during the exams).
2. Open to all males regardless of their background and social status.
3. Centered entirely on Confucian teachings (The Four Books and The Five Classics / The Nine Sacred Books).
