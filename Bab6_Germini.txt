BAB 6: PENINGKATAN TAMADUN YUNANI DAN ROM / CHAPTER 6: RISE OF THE GREEK AND ROMAN CIVILISATIONS  ================================================================================6.1 Tamadun Yunani: Lokasi dan Perkembangannya / Greek Civilisation: Location and Development  --- RINGKASAN / SUMMARY ---
• Tamadun Yunani berkembang di Semenanjung Greece dan pulau-pulau di Laut Aegean serta Laut Mediterranean antara 1,000 SM hingga 800 SM.
• Diketahui dengan pembentukan negara-kota atau polis yang merupakan gabungan tiga komponen: bandar utama, bandar kecil, dan kawasan kampung.
• Ciri utama polis termasuk Acropolis (pusat komuniti terlindung/kubu di lokasi strategik) dan Agora (kawasan lapang tempat pertemuan dan pasar awam).
• Tiga polis terkenal ialah Athens (paling ramai penduduk), Sparta (paling luas), dan Corinth.  • The Greek Civilisation developed in the Greece Peninsula and surrounding islands in the Aegean and Mediterranean Seas between 1,000 BC and 800 BC.
• Famous for city-states known as polis, which consisted of three components: main town, small town, and countryside villages.
• Key features of a polis include the Acropolis (a fortified hilltop community center) and the Agora (an open space used as a gathering place and marketplace).
• Three famous polis were Athens (most populated), Sparta (largest in area), and Corinth.  --- SOALAN OBJEKTIF / MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS ---Q1. • Athens mengalami 5 sistem pemerintahan: Monarki (pemerintahan beraja), Oligarki (pemerintahan oleh beberapa orang berkuasa), Aristokrasi (pemerintahan oleh golongan bangsawan), Tirani (pemerintahan oleh diktator/zalim), dan Demokrasi (pemerintahan oleh rakyat).
• Sistem demokrasi di Athens dipimpin oleh Dewan Perhimpunan (badan tertinggi), Majlis (Majlis 500), Majistret (kakitangan awam), dan Juri (badan pengadilan).
• Pericles memantapkan sistem demokrasi dengan memberi penekanan kepada kuasa rakyat.
• Sparta mengamalkan sistem Monarki Terhad (dipimpin oleh dua orang raja) dan memberikan penekanan yang sangat tinggi kepada disiplin ketenteraan serta latihan fizikal untuk lelaki dan wanita.  • Athens experienced 5 systems of government: Monarchy (rule by king), Oligarchy (rule by a few powerful individuals), Aristocracy (rule by nobility), Tyranny (rule by a dictator/tyrant), and Democracy (rule by the people).
• Athenian democracy was structured around the Assembly (highest body), the Council (Council of 500), Magistrates (civil servants), and the Juries (judicial body).
• Pericles strengthened democracy by emphasizing the power of citizens.
• Sparta practiced a Limited Monarchy (ruled by two kings) and placed extreme emphasis on military discipline and physical training for both men and women.  --- SOALAN OBJEKTIF / MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS ---Q1. • Tamadun Rom terletak di Lembah Latium, berhampiran Sungai Tiber.
• Terbahagi kepada tiga zaman utama: Zaman Beraja (753-509 SM), Zaman Republik (509-27 SM), dan Zaman Empayar (27 SM-476 M).
• Mencapai era kegemilangan Pax Romana (27 SM-180 M) yang membawa keamanan selama 200 tahun merentasi benua Asia, Afrika, dan Eropah.
• Masyarakat Rom terbahagi kepada tiga kelompok: Patrician (golongan atasan), Plebian (petani, artisan, peniaga), dan Hamba.  • Roman Civilisation was located in the Latium Valley, near the Tiber River.
• Divided into three main eras: Monarchy Era (753-509 BC), Republic Era (509-27 BC), and Empire Era (27 BC-476 AD).
• Reached its golden age during the Pax Romana (27 BC-180 AD), providing 200 years of peace across Asia, Africa, and Europe.
• Roman society was divided into three classes: Patricians (upper class), Plebeians (farmers, artisans, traders), and Slaves.  --- SOALAN OBJEKTIF / MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS ---Q1. • Seni bina Rom terkenal dengan ketahanan, saiz yang besar, keindahan estetika, dan reka bentuk yang unik (kubah bulat, arca, penggunaan simen/pozzolana).
• Marco Vitruvius merupakan arkitek terkenal yang mengarang buku "On Architecture".
• Binaan seni bina Rom yang terkenal:  Colosseum: Arena tempat pertunjukan gladiator (muat 50,000 orang).  Pantheon: Tempat penyembahan dewa-dewi yang mempunyai kubah terbesar dan ruang Oculus.  Amfiteater: Gelanggang terbuka untuk persembahan dan gladiator.  Akueduk: Sistem saliran air sistematik membekalkan air ke bandar dan tempat mandi.  Jalan Raya Rom: Jalan berlapis-lapis untuk kegunaan tentera dan pengangkutan.  Tembok Pertahanan: Melindungi empayar daripada serangan musuh (contoh: Tembok Hadrian).  Tempat Mandi Awam: Kemudahan kebersihan, riadah, dan sosial masyarakat (contoh: Caracalla).  • Roman architecture is known for its durability, massive size, aesthetic value, and creative designs (domes, arches, use of concrete/pozzolana).
• Marco Vitruvius was a famous architect who wrote "On Architecture".
• Major Roman architectural achievements:  Colosseum: Gladiatorial arena capable of seating 50,000 people.  Pantheon: Temple built for worshipping gods with a massive dome and an Oculus opening.  Amphitheatre: Open-air arena used for public performances and gladiator fights.  Aqueduct: Systematic water conveyance system supplying water to cities and baths.  Roman Roads: Multi-layered roads for military movement and transport.  Defensive Walls: Built to protect the empire from enemies (e.g., Hadrian's Wall).  Public Baths: Facilities built for cleanliness, recreation, and socialization (e.g., Baths of Caracalla).  --- SOALAN OBJEKTIF / MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS ---Q1.
