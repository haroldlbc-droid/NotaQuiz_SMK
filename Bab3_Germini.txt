TOPIK 3.1: Maksud Zaman Prasejarah / Topic 3.1: Meaning of the Prehistoric Age
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[RINGKASAN / SUMMARY]
BM: 
Zaman Prasejarah bermaksud zaman sebelum manusia mengetahui dan mengenal tulisan. 
Zaman ini dibahagikan kepada dua: Zaman Batu (Paleolitik, Mesolitik, Neolitik) 
dan Zaman Logam (gangsa dan besi).
- Paleolitik (2.5 juta - 8,000 SM): Menggunakan teknologi batu ringkas dan tulang haiwan.
- Mesolitik (12,000 - 4,000 SM): Memburu binatang dan menangkap ikan.
- Neolitik (10,000 - 2,000 SM): Petempatan menetap, bercucuk tanam, menternak binatang, dan membuat tembikar.
- Logam (3,500 SM - 500 M): Inovasi gangsa dan besi, pelayaran, serta perdagangan sistem barter.

EN: 
The Prehistoric Age refers to the period before humans knew and learned writing. 
It is divided into two main categories: the Stone Age (Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic) 
and the Metal Age (bronze and iron).
- Palaeolithic (2.5 million - 8,000 BC): Simple stone technology and animal bones.
- Mesolithic (12,000 - 4,000 BC): Hunting animals and catching fish.
- Neolithic (10,000 - 2,000 BC): Permanent settlements, farming, livestock breeding, and pottery making.
- Metal (3,500 BC - 500 AD): Bronze and iron innovation, seafaring, and barter trade.

[SOALAN SUBJEKTIF / SUBJECTIVE QUESTION]

EN: Explain two main differences between the Palaeolithic Age and the Neolithic Age in terms of technology and lifestyle.

Jawapan Cadangan / Suggested Answer:
- BM: Pada Zaman Paleolitik, manusia menggunakan peralatan batu yang kasar dan hidup secara nomad. Manakala pada Zaman Neolitik, peralatan batu lebih halus/kemas dan manusia mula hidup menetap serta bercucuk tanam.
- EN: In the Palaeolithic Age, humans used rough stone tools and lived a nomadic lifestyle. Meanwhile, in the Neolithic Age, stone tools were refined/polished and humans began living in permanent settlements and practicing agriculture.

TOPIK 3.2: Lokasi Zaman Prasejarah di Dunia dan Asia Tenggara / 
Topic 3.2: Locations of the Prehistoric Age in the World and Southeast Asia
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[RINGKASAN / SUMMARY]
BM: 
Lokasi prasejarah dikesan melalui penemuan artifak dan ekofak di kawasan terbuka (lembah sungai) atau tertutup (gua). 
- Di Dunia: Altamira (Sepanyol), Gua Chauvet (Perancis), Catal Huyuk (Turki), Stonehenge (Britain), Bhimbetka (India), Zhoukoudian (China), Shahr-i-Sokhta (Iran), Gobekli Tepe (Turki).
- Di Asia Tenggara: Lenggong (Malaysia), Ban Chiang (Thailand), Gua Liang Bua (Indonesia), Gua Padah-lin (Myanmar), Sa Hyun (Vietnam), Gua Tabon (Filipina).

EN: 
Prehistoric sites are identified through artifacts and ecofacts found in open areas (river valleys) or enclosed areas (caves).
- World Sites: Altamira (Spain), Chauvet Cave (France), Catal Huyuk (Turkey), Stonehenge (Britain), Bhimbetka (India), Zhoukoudian (China), Shahr-i-Sokhta (Iran), Gobekli Tepe (Turkey).
- Southeast Asia Sites: Lenggong (Malaysia), Ban Chiang (Thailand), Liang Bua Cave (Indonesia), Padah-lin Cave (Myanmar), Sa Hyun (Vietnam), Tabon Cave (Philippines).

TOPIK 3.3: Ciri-ciri Kehidupan Manusia Zaman Prasejarah / 
Topic 3.3: Characteristics of Human Life in the Prehistoric Age
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[RINGKASAN / SUMMARY]
BM: 
Ciri kehidupan meliputi tempat kediaman, peralatan, pengumpulan makanan, kegiatan ekonomi, organisasi sosial, kepercayaan, dan kesenian.
- Kediaman: Berpindah dari gua/ranting (Paleolitik) kepada rumah tetap batu/kayu (Neolitik/Logam).
- Ekonomi: Daripada sara diri/nomad (Paleolitik) kepada pertanian, penternakan, dan sistem barter (Neolitik/Logam).
- Kepercayaan: Mengamalkan animisme (Paleolitik), ritual/penyembahan alam dan kehidupan selepas mati (Neolitik/Logam).
- Kesenian: Lukisan gua realisme (Paleolitik) bertukar kepada corak simbolisme/geometri pada tembikar dan gendang gangsa (Neolitik/Logam).

EN: 
Life characteristics include dwelling places, tools, food gathering, economic activities, social organization, beliefs, and art.
- Dwellings: Shifted from caves/branches (Palaeolithic) to permanent stone/wooden houses (Neolithic/Metal).
- Economy: From self-subsistence/nomadic (Palaeolithic) to agriculture, livestock breeding, and barter system (Neolithic/Metal).
- Beliefs: Animism (Palaeolithic) shifted to rituals, nature worship, and belief in life after death (Neolithic/Metal).
- Art: Realism cave paintings (Palaeolithic) evolved into symbolism/geometric patterns on pottery and bronze drums (Neolithic/Metal).

TOPIK 3.4: Kesinambungan Sumbangan Zaman Prasejarah kepada Kemajuan Kehidupan Manusia / 
Topic 3.4: Continuity of Prehistoric Contributions to Human Progress
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[RINGKASAN / SUMMARY]
BM: 
Aktiviti prasejarah dikekalkan dan ditambah baik sehingga hari ini:
- Pertanian & Penternakan: Daripada sara diri menjadi industri komersial besar-besaran.
- Memburu Binatang: Daripada keperluan hidup menjadi hobi berlesen.
- Peralatan & Tembikar: Daripada batu/tulang kepada mesin moden; tembikar menjadi industri penting (cth: Labu Sayong).
- Ideofak & Monumen: Amalan penghormatan orang mati dikekalkan; seni bina megalitik mengilhami seni bina binaan moden (cth: KLCC).

EN: 
Prehistoric activities have been preserved and improved until today:
- Agriculture & Livestock: From self-subsistence to large-scale commercial industries.
- Animal Hunting: From survival necessity to a licensed hobby.
- Tools & Pottery: From stone/bones to modern machinery; pottery evolved into key industries (e.g., Labu Sayong).
- Ideofacts & Monuments: Respect for the deceased is maintained; megalithic architecture inspired modern structure designs (e.g., KLCC).

TOPIK 3.5: Zaman Prasejarah di Malaysia / Topic 3.5: Prehistoric Age in Malaysia
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[RINGKASAN / SUMMARY]
BM: 
Malaysia melalui Zaman Paleolitik (Kota Tampan, Gua Niah, Tingkayu), Hoabinhian/Epi-Paleolitik (Gua Cha, Jenderam Hilir), Neolitik (Gua Kecil, Lubang Angin), dan Logam (Lembah Bernam, Sg. Tembeling).
- Bukti Kewujudan: Penemuan peralatan batu/logam, tembikar (Gua Niah), manik (Kuala Selinsing), lukisan gua (Gua Niah/Tambun), ukiran batu (Batu Patung, Sarawak), dan budaya megalit (Pengkalan Kempas).
- Kaedah Pengebumian: Pengebumian memanjang (Gua Cha), melentur (Gua Gunung Runtuh), keranda kayu, tempayan, dan kubur kepingan batu.

EN: 
Malaysia went through Palaeolithic (Kota Tampan, Niah Cave, Tingkayu), Hoabinhian/Epi-Palaeolithic (Gua Cha, Jenderam Hilir), Neolithic (Gua Kecil, Lubang Angin), and Metal Age (Bernam Valley, Sg. Tembeling).
- Evidence of Existence: Discovery of stone/metal tools, pottery (Niah Cave), beads (Kuala Selinsing), cave paintings (Niah/Tambun Cave), stone carvings (Batu Patung, Sarawak), and megalithic culture (Pengkalan Kempas).
- Burial Methods: Extended burial (Gua Cha), flexed burial (Gua Gunung Runtuh), wooden boat/log coffin, jar burial, and stone slab graves.
