TOPIK 5.1: EMPAT LOKASI TAMADUN AWAL DUNIA
TOPIC 5.1: FOUR LOCATIONS OF EARLY WORLD CIVILISATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[RINGKASAN TOPIK / TOPIC SUMMARY]
(BM)
Empat tamadun awal dunia bermula dan berkembang di kawasan lembah sungai, iaitu:
1. Tamadun Mesopotamia - Sungai Tigris dan Sungai Euphrates.
2. Tamadun Mesir Purba - Sungai Nil.
3. Tamadun Indus - Sungai Indus.
4. Tamadun Huang He - Sungai Huang He (Sungai Kuning).
Sungai memainkan peranan penting sebagai sumber air bagi pertanian, kawasan petempatan, dan laluan perhubungan/pengangkutan.

(EN)
The four early world civilisations originated and developed in river valleys, namely:
1. Mesopotamian Civilisation - Tigris and Euphrates Rivers.
2. Ancient Egyptian Civilisation - Nile River.
3. Indus Civilisation - Indus River.
4. Huang He Civilisation - Huang He River (Yellow River).
Rivers played a vital role in providing water for agriculture, human settlements, and transportation/communication routes.

TOPIK 5.2: PERKEMBANGAN EMPAT TAMADUN AWAL DUNIA
TOPIC 5.2: DEVELOPMENT OF THE FOUR EARLY WORLD CIVILISATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[RINGKASAN TOPIK / TOPIC SUMMARY]
(BM)
- Tamadun Mesopotamia: Terletak di kawasan Bulan Sabit Subur. Bermula di Sumeria. Terkenal dengan pembinaan negara-kota seperti Ur dan struktur binaan zigurat.
- Tamadun Mesir Purba: Dikenali sebagai "Hadiah Sungai Nil". Terdiri daripada tiga tahap pemerintahan (Kerajaan Purba, Kerajaan Pertengahan, dan Kerajaan Baru) yang disatukan oleh Raja Mennes.
- Tamadun Indus: Terletak di Lembah Sungai Indus. Mempunyai dua bandar terancang yang terkenal iaitu Mohenjo-Daro dan Harappa dengan sistem perpaipan/pembetungan yang canggih.
- Tamadun Huang He: Terletak di kawasan utara China. Terkenal dengan kerajaan awal seperti Xia, Shang, dan Zhou serta pusat kerajaan di Anyang.

(EN)
- Mesopotamian Civilisation: Located in the Fertile Crescent. Began in Sumer. Known for city-states like Ur and ziggurat structures.
- Ancient Egyptian Civilisation: Known as "The Gift of the Nile". Consisted of three kingdom periods (Old, Middle, and New Kingdoms) unified by King Mennes.
- Indus Civilisation: Located in the Indus River Valley. Features two famous planned cities, Mohenjo-Daro and Harappa, with sophisticated plumbing/sewerage systems.
- Huang He Civilisation: Located in northern China. Known for early dynasties like Xia, Shang, and Zhou, with Anyang as a major centre.

TOPIK 5.3: PERBANDINGAN SUMBANGAN TAMADUN AWAL
TOPIC 5.3: COMPARISON OF CONTRIBUTIONS OF EARLY CIVILISATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[RINGKASAN TOPIK / TOPIC SUMMARY]
(BM)
Sumbangan tamadun awal merangkumi pelbagai aspek:
1. Pemerintahan & Pentadbiran: Kod Hammurabi (Mesopotamia), Firaun berkuasa mutlak (Mesir Purba), pemerintahan oleh golongan pendeta (Indus), dan konsep Mandat dari Syurga (Huang He).
2. Ekonomi: Perkembangan pertanian, perdagangan antarabangsa, dan pengkhususan pekerjaan.
3. Teknologi: Tulisan Kuneiform & papirus, penciptaan roda, bajak, kalendar (365 hari / berasaskan 12 bulan), perubatan, serta kompas magnetik.
4. Kesenian & Binaan: Zigurat, Piramid, Sfinks, ukiran batu, tembikar, dan puisi.

(EN)
Contributions of early civilisations span across multiple aspects:
1. Governance & Administration: Code of Hammurabi (Mesopotamia), absolute rule by Pharaohs (Ancient Egypt), governance by priests (Indus), and Mandate of Heaven (Huang He).
2. Economy: Development of agriculture, international trade, and job specialisation.
3. Technology: Cuneiform script & papyrus paper, invention of the wheel, plough, calendars (365-day / 12-month based), medicine, and magnetic compass.
4. Arts & Architecture: Ziggurats, Pyramids, Sphinx, stone carvings, pottery, and poetry.
